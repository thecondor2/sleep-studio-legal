Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Sleep Studio is a sleep and relaxation application offering relaxing sounds, sleep-related tools, and optional premium features.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, Sleep Studio may process app preferences, sleep-related information, device identifiers, advertising information, and subscription status.

2. Advertising

Sleep Studio uses Google AdMob to provide advertising, including optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its partners may process device identifiers and other information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes, subject to applicable consent requirements.

Google Privacy Policy

3. Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. RevenueCat is used to manage subscription status and entitlements. Payment details are handled by Google Play rather than collected directly by Sleep Studio.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage

Sleep Studio may store app preferences and sleep-related information locally on your device. Third-party services may process technical and transaction-related information needed to provide their services.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

You may choose not to watch optional rewarded ads. You can manage subscriptions through Google Play. Where applicable, you may also manage advertising consent choices through the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Sleep Studio is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when our features, practices, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be available on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer using the contact details provided on Sleep Studio's Google Play listing.
